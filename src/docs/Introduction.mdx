# mcp-apps-ui

React components and Tailwind tokens for building [MCP Apps](https://claude.com/docs/connectors/building/mcp-apps/design-guidelines) that render inside Claude.

> **Independent project.** Not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Anthropic PBC. "Claude" and "Anthropic" are trademarks of Anthropic PBC.

The library gives you three things:

- **Tokens** that read the style variables Claude injects into your app, exposed as Tailwind utilities such as `bg-surface`, `text-secondary` and `heading-md`. Light and dark come from the host.
- **Components** shaped by the design guidelines. Every control is visible, keyboard operable and at least 44px tall.
- **Guard rails.** Lint rules and tests that fail when something floats, scrolls vertically, or hardcodes a colour.

## Why not a general component library?

The guidelines rule out dropdowns, context menus, popovers and floating panels. They get clipped by the frame your app lives in and fight the host's z-index. Most React libraries are built around exactly those layers. This one has none of them, so the right choice is also the easy one: a `SegmentedControl` instead of a select, `ToggleChips` instead of a filter menu, `InlineTabs` instead of a drill-in.
